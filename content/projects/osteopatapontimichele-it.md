This website was custom designed and developed with the aim of improving the online presence and effectively and directly presenting the treatments offered and the doctor's experience.

Given that the target customer base is also made up of older and less tech-savvy people, it was important that the site and its features, such as appointment booking, were intuitive and easy to use.

With the aim of maximizing the speed of the site, in addition to developing it custom using all the best practices for speed and accessibility, it is hosted on servers and data centers located in Italy, obtaining maximum PageSpeed scores.
