New project of the renowned Italian pub / restaurant / summer disco "Ralph's Oltre il Pub" in the heart of Ibiza, for which I created the website in 2019 ([link](https://nicolasguarini.it/projects/ralpholtreilpub-it)).

The site was created with Wordpress in a completely custom way, maintaining part of the existing brand identity of the restaurant in Italy, and limiting the use of external plugins as much as possible.  
The site is multilingual Italian / English / Spanish, created with maximum attention to accessibility and performance.
